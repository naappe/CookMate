Singapore-Style Black Pepper Prawns

Black pepper is the dominant character, supported by a garlic-ginger-shallot base, savoury sauces, restrained sweetness, butter and a light glossy thickening.

Main400 g prawns
Yield2–3 portions
Prep15 min
Cook8–10 min
HeatHigh → Medium

Ingredients

Prawns, cleaned400 g
Black peppercorns10 g
Cooking oil2 tbsp
Salt½ tsp
Butter1 tbsp
Garlic20 g
Ginger5 g
Shallots40 g
Dry red chillies2–3
Curry leaves1 sprig
Dark soy sauce1 tbsp
Oyster sauce1 tbsp
Tomato ketchup1 tbsp
Worcestershire1 tsp
Cornflour + water1 tsp + 2 tbsp

Shopping List

Flavour Architecture

Primary: coarsely crushed black pepper.
Aromatic base: garlic + ginger + shallots.
Savoury anchor: oyster sauce + dark soy.
Balance: ketchup + Worcestershire.
Finish: butter + cornflour slurry for gloss and coating.

Cooking Method

1. Mise en place. Clean and dry prawns. Chop aromatics. Crush pepper. Mix slurry.
2. Sear. Heat a wide pan over high heat. Quickly sear prawns and remove before overcooking.
3. Aromatics. Reduce to medium. Add oil and butter, then shallots, garlic and ginger. Add chilli and curry leaves.
4. Sauce. Add pepper, soy, oyster sauce, ketchup and Worcestershire.
5. Glaze. Return prawns. Add slurry gradually and toss only until glossy and coated.
6. Serve. Check seasoning and serve immediately with fried rice or noodles.
