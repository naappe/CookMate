Visual cooking tutorial

Use this sequence as a general guide. Your selected cooking plan below will tell you which steps apply.

1. PrepareCut the main ingredient evenly. Measure seasonings before cooking so the process stays controlled.
2. Season or marinateCoat evenly with the recommended combination. Do not add every seasoning you own; each ingredient should have a purpose.
3. Brown or build the baseFor pan, grill and stew, browning develops deeper flavour. For soup and curry, start with onion, garlic and other aromatics.
4. CookUse the selected method and cook until the centre is properly done. Time changes with thickness, portion size and appliance.
5. Sauce and finishBuild sauce separately or from cooked pan juices. Fresh acid, herbs and butter are often best added near the end.
6. Taste and correctTaste before adding more. Correct one problem at a time: bland, heavy, spicy, sour, thin or lacking freshness.
